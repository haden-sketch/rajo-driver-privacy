Rajo Driver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Rajo Driver is a private workforce application published by HK Global Ventures LLC ("we", "us") for drivers of Rajo Transportation LLC ("Rajo"), the carrier whose dispatch system the app connects to. It is provided to people who drive for Rajo Transportation as a tool for doing their job. It is not a consumer product and is not intended for use by the general public.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how long we keep it.

The short version. The app collects your location — including while the app is closed or the screen is off — so that dispatch knows where your truck is and can give customers accurate arrival times. It also collects the documents you photograph, the messages you send through the app, and basic information about your work assignments. All of it goes to Rajo Transportation's own systems. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who this applies to

This app is used by drivers and contractors engaged by Rajo Transportation LLC. You must sign in with a phone number or set of credentials that we have issued to you. There is no public or anonymous mode.

3. Background location — what it means in practice

The app asks for permission to access your location all the time, not only while the app is open. This is the core function of the app and it needs explaining plainly:

  • While you are on a load, the app records your position periodically even if your phone is locked and in a cupholder. Without this, dispatch would lose the truck every time the screen turned off, and arrival times given to customers would be guesses.
  • While tracking is active, Android shows a permanent notification reading that location sharing is on. That notification is required by Android and by us — it exists so that tracking is never silent. If it is not in your notification shade, the app is not tracking you.
  • When your phone has no signal, positions are stored on the device and uploaded when signal returns. Nothing is collected that would not have been collected while online.
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in Android Settings. The app will keep working for messages and documents, but dispatch will no longer see your position and features that depend on it will stop.

4. Where the data goes

Data from the app is sent to systems operated by or on behalf of Rajo Transportation LLC. Specifically:

RecipientWhat it receivesWhy
Rajo Transportation's own dispatch system Everything described in section 2 It is the system this app exists to feed.
Our hosting and database providers (Railway, Supabase) The same data, stored on our behalf They host our servers, database and document storage.
Mapbox and HERE Location coordinates, at the moment a map or route is drawn To render maps and calculate truck-legal routes.
Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) A push token and notification contents To deliver dispatch alerts to your phone.
Twilio Your phone number To send the one-time code that signs you in.

We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising or ad targeting. We do not build advertising profiles. The only people who see your location are Rajo Transportation staff who need it to dispatch and pay you, and customers who receive an arrival estimate for their own shipment.

5. How long we keep it

Location history, documents, inspection records and pay records are retained for as long as we need them to run the business and to satisfy the recordkeeping rules that apply to a motor carrier — in practice, several years for records tied to loads, pay, fuel tax and inspections. Diagnostic data is kept only as long as it is useful for fixing a problem.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in token is held in the Android encrypted keystore, not in ordinary app storage. Access to the dispatch system is restricted to authorised Rajo Transportation accounts.

7. Your choices and your rights

  • Permissions. Location, camera, photo and notification permissions can each be changed or revoked at any time in Android Settings.
  • Access and correction. You may ask us what we hold about you and ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Deletion. You may ask us to delete your data. We will do so except where we are required to keep a record — for example, records tied to pay, fuel tax, inspections or an incident. We will tell you which category applies rather than refuse without explanation.

8. Children

This app is a workplace tool for licensed commercial drivers. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or who receives it, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
