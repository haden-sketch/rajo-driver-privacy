Rajo Driver — Support

Published by HK Global Ventures LLC

Rajo Driver is a private workforce app for drivers dispatched by the carrier that set up their account. It is not a consumer product. If you drive for that carrier, your dispatcher is the fastest way to get help with a load, a stop, or your paperwork.

For questions about the app itself — signing in, the map, uploading documents, location tracking, or deleting your account — email hassan@hkglobalventures.com and we will reply by email.

Signing in

The app signs you in with the phone number your carrier set up and a one-time text code. If the code does not arrive, ask your dispatcher to confirm the number on file.

Location

The app records your position only while a load is in progress, and shows a persistent notification whenever it is doing so. You can turn location off in your phone's settings at any time.

Deleting your account

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete my account. The request goes to your carrier, who removes your sign-in, your name and phone number on your driver profile, and your saved app settings. Records the carrier is required by law to keep — hours-of-service logs, settlements and pay records, signed delivery paperwork, and safety events — are retained under their policy.

Privacy

How the app handles your data is described in the privacy policy.
